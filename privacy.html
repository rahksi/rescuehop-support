Rescue Hop privacy notice

Rescue Hop is developed by Christian Nicolini. For support or privacy requests, contact rescuehop.support@gmail.com.

Progress on your device

The game stores collections, discoveries, coins, equipped cosmetics, purchase entitlements and personal bests locally. You don't need a Rescue Hop account to play. We don't operate a player-account database or an automatic first-party gameplay analytics service.

Local progress can be lost if you delete the app or change devices. Restoring an Apple purchase restores an eligible purchase entitlement, not your coin balance or sanctuary collection. Don't delete the app as a troubleshooting step before making sure you can recover what you need.

Optional playtest notes

If you switch on local run recording, Rescue Hop keeps up to 64 recent run summaries on your device. These include the forest, active run time, height, pickups, deliveries and death cause. They do not include your name, Apple ID or advertising identifier.

Recording is off by default. You can clear the notes in Settings or choose to copy and share them. Copying does not send them to us automatically.

Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center, Apple handles authentication and the game can submit your best height to the selected forest's leaderboard. Game Center use is optional. Apple's handling of its account and gaming information is described in Apple's Game Center privacy information.

Purchases

Apple processes real-money payments. Rescue Hop receives purchase and restore results used to grant and maintain the items or benefits you bought. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Apple handles purchase history and payment information under its privacy policy.

Purchases use Unity In-App Purchasing as well as Apple. Unity may process installation, device, session and purchase-related information to provide its purchasing service. The information processed depends on which purchasing features are enabled. We do not ask you to provide an email address to make an in-game Apple purchase. See Unity IAP privacy information.

Advertising and privacy choices

Some playtest versions do not serve ads. Versions with advertising enabled use Unity LevelPlay and ironSource Ads for optional cosmetic-coin bonuses and occasional ads between runs. We do not add advertising banners to the gameplay screen.

These services can process device identifiers, ad views and interactions, and performance information to provide and measure advertising, prevent fraud and support their services. Tracking permission and privacy choices can affect how information is used. Read Unity's privacy policy and its ironSource data disclosure. We will update this notice before introducing additional advertising providers or materially changing these practices.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before advertising requests. Its SDK processes approximate location to determine applicable privacy rules, along with consent interactions and performance information. This does not require GPS permission; the game does not request precise location access. Google's privacy policy describes Google's processing. In versions with advertising enabled, required privacy choices are available from the game's Settings. Apple's tracking prompt is separate from these choices; you can change tracking permission in your iPhone's Settings. Declining optional tracking does not prevent ordinary gameplay.

The one-time Supporter purchase removes both optional rewarded ads and between-run ads. It does not remove processing needed for purchases or Game Center use you choose. A feature described here may be unavailable in a playtest version; the game will show whether it is available before you use it.

When you contact us

If you send us an email, we receive your address and whatever information you choose to include, such as device details, screenshots or copied run notes. We use that information to answer and investigate your request. Please avoid sending passwords, verification codes or unnecessary personal information.

Retention and requests

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed or replaced. Clearing local playtest notes does not clear your game progress or any information already sent to us. We retain support correspondence only as needed to address the request and legitimate recordkeeping obligations. Contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us; applicable rights depend on your location.

Requests about information held independently by Apple, Google or advertising partners may also need to go to those providers. We do not promise that clearing local data deletes information held by another company.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to certain processing, or opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising. Contact us using the address above for information we hold, or use the available in-game privacy controls for advertising choices. We do not use your support email address for targeted advertising. You may also raise a concern with your local privacy authority.

Children

Rescue Hop is intended for a general audience and is not primarily directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and respond.

Website

These support pages do not add advertising scripts, analytics scripts or contact forms. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when a visitor is not signed in. Read GitHub’s privacy statement for its data handling and your choices.

Changes

We will update this notice if the game's data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

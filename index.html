Need a helping paw?

Questions, a troublesome jump, or something that doesn't look right? Get in touch.

rescuehop.support@gmail.com

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version and what happened. A screenshot helps. Don't send passwords, verification codes or payment-card details.

Your animals and progress

Friends still with you at each 2,000 m delivery go home to the sanctuary. A delivery protects those animals; it doesn't save your height or create a respawn.

Bank 100 friends in Woodland to open Mushroom, then 100 in Mushroom to open Rainforest.

Purchases and restores

Use Restore Apple purchases in the game's Settings to restore eligible one-time purchases under the Apple account that bought them.

Apple purchase restores do not restore a local coin balance or sanctuary collection. Contact us before deleting the app if you're trying to recover progress.

Something feels unfair?

Tell us which forest, roughly how high you were, and what hit you. If you choose to enable local playtest notes, you can copy them from Settings and include them in your message.

Those notes are not uploaded automatically. Only share them if you want to.

Privacy questions

Use the same email address for questions about your information. You can read our privacy notice for details about local progress and connected services.

Ordinary gameplay doesn't require a Rescue Hop account.
